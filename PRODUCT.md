# Tension Lab Fitness

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People considering strength coaching with Sam in Long Beach, California.

## Product Purpose

Explain the coaching, show genuine client experiences and results, and help prospective clients start an inquiry.

## Operating Context

Sam trains at a general gym. Tension Lab Fitness is the coaching brand, not a private gym owned by the trainer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Extend the existing static HTML site with Home, Coaching, Results, About, and Contact pages.
- The user will publish through GitHub Actions. Provide a GitHub Pages workflow without publishing on their behalf.
- Contact details may remain an explicitly marked placeholder, as authorized by the user.
- The draft inquiry flow prepares text locally. No booking service, application backend, payment, or message delivery has been connected.
- Pricing, credentials, full biography, trainer portrait, exact gym access requirements, and a real contact destination remain unconfirmed.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Tension Lab Fitness. Coach: Sam. Location: Long Beach.
- Premium quality inspired by Ultimate Performance.
- Pure white, black, and gray; no blue, orange, cream, or taupe surfaces.
- The user approved normal-width Helvetica Neue after rejecting condensed typography.
- Short, factual language. Avoid generic slogans, repeated explanations, and fabricated promises.
- A homepage workout photograph is welcome. It should not imply that the pictured stock model is Sam or a client.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing Results page and four Google-review client photographs in `assets/`.
- Jimmy reports a 50 lb change from 235 to 185 lb, a tailored program, and competitive scores on a special operations fitness test.
- Damian reports nearly 100 lb lost and learning enough to continue his progress and help others with fitness.
- Genuine reviews from Ryan, Alex, Laura, Julia, and Ashley describe strength progress, teaching, follow-up, and guidance.
- Original review attribution URLs are retained in the site and `ASSETS.md`.
- The existing Google rating is a content snapshot of 5.0 from 7 reviews, not a live feed.

## Product Principles

- Connect each client result to an understandable coaching benefit.
- Use actual evidence rather than invented credentials, metrics, prices, or outcomes.
- Explain what contact actions do and distinguish a prepared inquiry from a sent message.
- Keep ordinary links and essential content usable without JavaScript.
