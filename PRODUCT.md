# Tension Lab Fitness

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People considering strength coaching with Sam in Long Beach, California.

## Product Purpose

Explain the coaching, show genuine client experiences and results, and help prospective clients start an inquiry.

## Operating Context

Sam trains at a general gym. Tension Lab Fitness is the coaching brand, not a private gym owned by the trainer. Visitors confirm the location and any gym access requirements with Sam.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The static HTML site includes Home, Method, Coaching, Results, About, Contact, and a 404 page.
- The user explicitly requested putting the site on GitHub. The existing public repository at https://github.com/habonnur/tension-lab-fitness and its GitHub Pages deployment are authorized destinations.
- The public business phone is (408) 202-7994, stored as `+14082027994` in `assets/site-config.js`. It was verified against the [Google business listing](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Tension+Lab+Fitness/data=!4m2!3m1!1s0x0:0xe7cc62888031453c) on October 12, 2026.
- Visitors can call Sam or prepare an inquiry locally, then open a prefilled SMS draft in their messaging app or copy the text. The visitor reviews and sends the message. The website does not save inquiry details or send messages.
- Name and goals are required for a draft. Visitor email, training experience, and availability are optional. Sam's contact email is currently empty; email actions stay hidden until a valid address is configured.
- No booking service, application backend, payment, or automatic message delivery is connected.
- Pricing, credentials, full biography, trainer portrait, and exact gym access requirements remain unconfirmed. Sam's email and any booking link are optional and unprovided.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand name: Tension Lab Fitness. Coach: Sam. Location: Long Beach.
- Premium quality inspired by Ultimate Performance.
- Latest user-confirmed direction (2026-10-12): “Use this chat’s condensed-heading mockup,” superseding the earlier pure-white and all-Helvetica draft.
- Near-black and crisp white surfaces, Anton display type, Barlow Condensed navigation and controls, and readable Helvetica body copy. No orange or blue CTA.
- Short, factual language. Avoid fabricated promises and unsupported qualifications or outcomes.
- The homepage campaign image is a generated training illustration. It is not a real client, Sam, a result, or a photograph of the actual training location.
- The About workout image is stock photography with visible Rodrigo Rodrigues / Unsplash credit. It is not a portrait of Sam or a client.

## Evidence on Hand

- Four original Google-review client photographs are preserved in `assets/`. The user confirmed Sam has permission to use them; no generated modifications were made to client result imagery.
- Jimmy reports a 50 lb change from 235 to 185 lb, a tailored program, and competitive scores on a special operations fitness test.
- Damian reports nearly 100 lb lost and learning enough to continue his progress and help others with fitness.
- Genuine reviews from Ryan, Alex, Laura, Julia, and Ashley describe strength progress, teaching, follow-up, and guidance.
- Original review attribution URLs, asset origins, font licenses, and the phone source are recorded in `ASSETS.md`.

## Product Principles

- Connect each client result to an understandable coaching benefit.
- Use actual evidence rather than invented credentials, metrics, prices, or outcomes.
- Explain what contact actions do and distinguish a prepared inquiry from a sent message.
- Keep ordinary links and essential content usable without JavaScript; phone contact remains available when draft preparation is unavailable.
